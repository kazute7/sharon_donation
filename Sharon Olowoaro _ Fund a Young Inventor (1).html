Three inventions, many more to come

Each one starts with a problem I see around me: food that spoils, notes that need paper, and leaves that pile up. So I decided to build the fix myself.

Flexi Paper

A foldable digital notebook that works like normal paper but needs no ink or paper.

  • Write with a stylus or your finger
  • Saves notes to a microSD memory card
  • Switch it off and your notes stay
  • Charges with a Type-C cable

Leaf Sucker

A small machine that cleans up and makes manure for plants.

  • Suck: pulls dry fallen leaves off the ground like a vacuum
  • Grind: blades cut the leaves into small pieces
  • Manure: the ground leaves go straight onto plants
  • Cleans the compound without stress
  • Saves money, no need to buy fertilizer
  • Good for the environment

Evaporative Powered Fridge

A small fridge that uses a solar panel, a capacitor and a Peltier module.

  • The solar panel charges the capacitor
  • The capacitor powers the Peltier
  • The Peltier makes it cold inside
  • It recharges itself from the sun

About me, Sharon

I am a young inventor from Kogi State. I grew up in a family that taught me to be creative and independent early.

My love for creativity started when I was five. I loved watching creative cartoons, and I would spend hours drawing what I saw. Drawing became my own world.

Everything changed when I joined the Access programme. They taught us entrepreneurship: how to turn ideas into something real. That was where I first thought of making a fridge that uses water to keep things cold.

From that small idea, I never stopped. I started building, learning, failing, and building again. Today, that same passion has led me to create the Flexi Paper, the Leaf Sucker, and my solar-powered fridge.

This website was made possible because someone believed in my dream and sponsored it. I am sharing my story to inspire other young girls like me: you can create your own future.
